# SignalPrep resume entry

## Project title

**SignalPrep — Grounded GenAI Interview Practice Platform**

## Resume paragraph

Built a full-stack grounded GenAI interview-practice platform using Python, FastAPI, React, Ollama,
Pydantic, and hybrid BGE/TF-IDF retrieval, with resume/JD ingestion, evidence-cited questions,
structured feedback, adaptive follow-ups, and downloadable coaching reports. Implemented native
schema-constrained generation, citation validation, prompt-injection quarantine, auditable score
calibration, and explicit degraded-mode provenance. Evaluated Qwen 2.5 3B on a versioned synthetic
suite, achieving 100% Recall@3, 0.925 MRR, 100% schema/citation validity, and correct strong-over-weak
ranking across all three answer pairs.

## Interview boundary

Always say that the benchmark is synthetic and small. Do not claim human score agreement, hiring
validity, production deployment, or universal prompt-injection safety. Mention that absolute score-band
accuracy was 66.7% and median/p95 generation latency was 13.36/29.07 seconds when discussing limitations.
